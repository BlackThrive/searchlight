Assumptions

Resident Census population is an exposure choice. It may differ from the people present or exposed to police activity. Missing-ethnicity allocations are stated scenarios. Their ranges are not confidence intervals and are not combined with sampling uncertainty. Spatial smoothing stabilises under its model assumptions; inspect unsmoothed data, local changes and convergence diagnostics.

  • Resident Census 2021 exposure is held fixed over the submitted months.

Limitations

Repeated events, deployment, activity, selection and measurement can affect comparisons. Hit rates condition on being searched and do not by themselves establish discrimination. Darkness associations require assumptions about visibility and comparable activity and deployment. The method originated for vehicle stops; its transfer to pedestrian searches requires justification. Neither an undefined result nor a wide interval establishes absence of disparity.

  • Rates, bounds and count regression cover four example MSOAs selected for many events.
  • Outcome summaries use all 4,657 supplied events; these analyses have different scopes.
  • All three requested Dyfed-Powys submissions are missing.
